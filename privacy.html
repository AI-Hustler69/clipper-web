Privacy Policy

Clipper ("the tool") uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Who uses the tool

The tool is used only by its owner to manage the owner's own YouTube channels. It has no other users and no public sign-up.

What data is accessed

  • Information about the owner's channels and videos: titles, descriptions, tags, privacy status, scheduled times, playlists, thumbnails and captions.
  • Statistics and YouTube Analytics reports of the owner's own videos (views, likes, watch time, retention).
  • Public information about popular videos (titles and view counts) for occasional niche research.

The tool does not access, collect or store personal data of viewers or of any other YouTube users.

How data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to prepare and schedule the owner's own videos and to evaluate their performance.
  • OAuth tokens and fetched data are stored only on the owner's computer and in the owner's private repository. They are never sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.
  • Statistics of the owner's own videos are refreshed on every run. Public data about other channels (niche research) is not kept longer than 30 days without being refreshed, and is deleted when no longer needed.
  • No cookies or tracking are used by the tool or by this website.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. After revocation the tool can no longer access the channel. To delete stored data, the owner deletes the local token and data files; a deletion request can also be sent to the contact below and is handled within 7 days.
